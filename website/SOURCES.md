Address coordinates come from the US Census geocoder. Raw results and request URLs are in assets/coordinate-sources.json. They approximate address positions, not building entrances. The Allentown address returned both east/west alternatives; the displayed downtown point is marked approximate, not a verified entrance. The proposed AI Center location is a hypothetical classroom placement, not a geocoded real building.
